# Product overview
TheWinterArc is a private, local-first personal health and human-performance research workstation for Fedora Linux. Existing applications remain the systems of record. The desktop consumes their data to explain trends, workload, consistency and relationships.
## Scope
Body composition, strength, muscle workload, cardio and sports, sleep, nutrition, mobility, mood, habits, progress photographs, correlation lab and reports. Visual-first, silver-white/graphite design with dark mode. Single user; no mobile app.
## Non-goals
No exercise planning library, workout logger, food diary, mood diary, sleep diary or habit completion UI. Manual imports, metadata corrections and progress photos are allowed. No clinical diagnosis, injury prediction, mandatory cloud or subscription.
## Success
Reliable acquisition and provenance precede dashboard polish. Missing data is explicit; derived metrics are explainable and never presented as clinical measurements.
